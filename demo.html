Am I exam-ready?

An 8-minute check that tells an A1 German learner whether to book their Goethe or ÖSD exam, before they pay the fee.

What the assignment asked for

    Why this, and how we'd know it works

    The problem
    Learners finish A1 and book the exam without knowing if they're ready. A fail costs the full fee again and weeks on their journey abroad.
    How “ready” is decided
    Anchored on the exam's 60% pass mark. Ready = 75%+ overall and every skill at 60%. The margin covers lucky answers in a short check. 60–74% is Almost; under 60% is Not yet.
    How we'd know it works
    Follow learners who take the check and then the real exam. Of those marked Ready, how many pass? Tune the 75% until “Ready” reliably means “passes”.
    What it can't do yet
    No listening, writing or speaking questions. Learners rate those themselves, and it never changes the score.
